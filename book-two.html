Book two · coloring job

Boofo’s First Vacation

Every illustration in the second book, page by page, for colorists quoting the job. The line art is finished; it needs coloring to match book one.

Design system Boofo color model Cast Coloring method Palette All pages

The job

Color the finished black-and-white line art digitally so it looks hand-colored with marker, watercolor and colored pencil, matching book one. The story follows Boofo from the North Pole to Joey’s yard and back, through spring, summer, fall and winter.

  • 29illustrations
  • 6small · one figure
  • 15medium · 2–3 figures or a setting
  • 8large · full scene

Before you quote

Style and colors

Everything is defined in the design system: Boofo’s color model, the returning cast, the coloring method and the full palette. Boofo, Santa, Mr. Bim, Mr. Bam, Mr. Boom, the reindeer and the sleigh keep their book-one colors.

New for book two

  • Seasons book one never showed: spring, summer, fall, night and Christmas morning.
  • New characters whose colors will be agreed before work starts: Joey (11 pages), a black cat, three raccoons, a robin, a bee and ants.

Files and lettering

  • Line art is scanned at 300 dpi on letter-size pages and is sent privately to shortlisted colorists.
  • The cover title and the song lyrics on pages 21 and 24 are drawn into the art and get colored with it.
  • The typed story text on each page will be reset separately.
