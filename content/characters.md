# Character guide

## Boofo

### Who he is

Boofo is a stray puppy. Santa finds him shivering under a mound of snow, with "two large and very sad eyes", and the elves call him a "scrawny mutt". Once he is warm and fed he is never still, always running and jumping. He meets a shouted "Boo!" with playful barks. He licks faces, and he dozes in odd places, like a workbench where an elf nearly glues a fifth leg on him. He is also brave and useful. He finds Mr. Bim buried in a snowstorm, raises the alarm when the workshop catches fire, and carries glue pots, paint and Santa's lists for the elves. He barks to approve a good child's letter and whimpers at a bad one. In the end he rides in the sleigh and holds the reins.

Draw and color him as a puppy: eager, comic, and full of feeling.

### Build

- **Proportions**: a puppy. The head is about a third to two-fifths of the body length. The barrel body is compact, the haunches are round and the four legs are short and sturdy.
- **Head**: a rounded skull with a short, square muzzle that pushes forward. From the front, the white muzzle and cheeks make a wide peanut shape that puffs out past the brown of the head.
- **Ears**: the thing everyone remembers. They are very long, strap-shaped and **solid black**, set high on the sides of the skull. When he sits, they hang to the floor. When he runs, they stream straight back, longer than his whole body. In a tumble they whip and knot over his face. Treat the ears as an extra limb that shows his mood: drooping means sad or worried, flying means speed or joy, tangled means chaos.
- **Crown tuft**: three to five spiky black ink strands on top of the head, between the ears.
- **Eyes**: large upright ovals of bare paper, set close together at the top of the muzzle, with round black pupils. Black brow arcs float just above them and do most of the acting. Raised means joy or surprise. Pinched down toward the nose means anger. Tilted up in the middle means worry.
- **Nose**: a rounded black button at the end of the muzzle, with a white glint.
- **Muzzle**: white, with three or four tiny ink freckle dots on each side. The mouth line curls up into the cheeks. When the mouth is open, the inside is red and the tongue is salmon pink; the tongue often hangs out or licks.
- **Chest bib**: white, running from the chin down the chest onto the belly. Where it meets the brown, its edge breaks into two or three jagged fur points.
- **Legs and paws**: brown legs with white paws on all four feet, like socks. Mark the toes with two or three short ink lines. When a foot is turned up, show a large dark pad and three or four toe pads.
- **Tail**: short, carried upright with a slight curve. It is brown with a white, tufted, flame-shaped tip.
- **Size against the cast**: standing, Boofo comes up to an elf's knee. Sitting, his crown reaches an elf's hip. He fits easily on Santa's lap.

### Color map

| Area | Token | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Coat: head, back, sides, legs, tail | `boofo-coat` | Base layer, everywhere brown |
| Coat shadow | `boofo-coat-shadow` | Under the jaw, belly line, far legs, behind the haunch, under the ears |
| Coat accents | `boofo-coat-deep` | Tight creases only |
| Coat light | `boofo-coat-light` | Crown, cheeks, tops of shoulder and haunch |
| Rim light | `boofo-coat-glow` | Warm scenes only (workshop, fire, fireside) |
| Muzzle, cheeks, bib, belly, paws, tail tip | `boofo-white` (paper) | Leave as paper wherever you can |
| Shading on the whites | `boofo-white-shadow` | Under the chin, inside the bib edge, far paws |
| Ears, crown tuft, nose, pupils, brows | `ink-line` | Solid black; add a thin white glint along the top edge of an ear |
| Paw pads | `boofo-pad` with `boofo-pad-light` | Only when the feet face up |
| Mouth inside | `boofo-mouth` | Red |
| Tongue | `boofo-tongue` with `boofo-tongue-light` | Salmon, lighter than the mouth |

### Coloring Boofo, step by step

1. Fill every brown area flat with `boofo-coat`. Leave the white markings as paper.
2. Add `boofo-coat-shadow` in marker strokes that follow the form: the underside of the head and jaw, the belly, the inner and far legs, and the back of the haunch. Let some stroke edges show.
3. Touch in `boofo-coat-deep` in the tightest creases only, like the jaw line and where a leg meets the body.
4. Add `boofo-coat-light` on the top planes: the crown, the cheek pouches and the tops of the shoulder and haunch. A few short pencil-like flicks along the fur direction finish it.
5. Fill the ears, tuft and nose solid black. Scratch a thin paper-white glint along the top curve of each ear and on the nose.
6. Shade the white markings lightly with `boofo-white-shadow`, mostly under the chin and where the bib turns under.
7. Leave a `halo-gap` of white paper all round his contour wherever a background wash meets him.

### Lighting his coat by scene

- **Snowy day**: true chestnut. Keep shadows on the cool, brown side and let the snow's blue light his underside a little.
- **Workshop or house interior**: warmer. Push `boofo-coat-light` and add a touch of `boofo-coat-glow` on the rims.
- **Firelight**: strongest glow on the side facing the flames. Add `fire-orange` reflected onto his white bib.
- **Dusk**: slightly cooler and darker overall. Never let the coat go grey.

### Expressions and poses in book one

- *Happy, front view* (cover): a big open grin, tongue showing, one paw raised.
- *Startled, jumping back* (page 24): leaping back from a jack-in-the-box, mouth wide open, front paws flung up, ears thrown into the air, with ink wobble lines around him.
- *Worried* (page 20): brows tilted up, mouth small and open, ears hanging straight down.
- *Sad, sitting* (page 27): head low, ears pooled on the floor, pupils looking up.
- *Barking, angry* (page 13): brows pinched, teeth showing, braced on all fours.
- *Curious, walking in profile* (page 25): nose forward, tail up, ears hanging.
- *Running* (pages 25, 29 and 32): ears streaming straight back, body stretched, with ink speed lines.
- *Tumbling* (page 12): legs splayed, pads showing, ears knotted over his face, tongue out.
- *Busy helper* (page 28): standing on his hind legs with a hammer and brush in his mouth and a glue pot on his head.
- *Asleep* (pages 7 and 35): eyes closed as curved lines, curled in a basket under a patchwork quilt.

### Don't

- Don't color the ears brown or dark brown. They are black ink.
- Don't shift the coat toward orange, gold or tan. Lighting can warm it, but the base stays `boofo-coat`. The reindeer are the lighter, tanner animals.
- Don't move the white markings. They are always on the muzzle, cheeks, bib, belly, four paws and tail tip.
- Don't add a collar or clothes. In book one, things he wears are only gags, like the glue pot. The story says Mr. Bam lends him his hat for the sleigh ride, but the art never shows it. If book two keeps the hat, settle its color once and keep it the same on every page.

## Santa Claus

- **Build**: big, round and grandfatherly, with a full white beard, a curled moustache, bushy white brows and white hair. His eyes are often closed in a laugh.
- **Face**: `skin-base` with a strong `skin-blush` on his cheeks and a red nose.
- **Suit**, outdoors and in the sleigh: `santa-red`, with `santa-red-light` on the folds and `santa-red-deep` in the creases. The fur trim is paper with `paper-shade`. He has a black belt with a `santa-buckle` buckle, `santa-leather` mittens and black boots.
- **Workshop and house clothes**: a white shirt with rolled sleeves, `santa-trouser-red` trousers and suspenders, white socks and `santa-leather` brown shoes.
- **Sleigh**: body in `santa-red`, seat in `santa-sleigh-green`, runners in `santa-leather` scrollwork.

## The three elf brothers

They are always together and easy to tell apart by build, hair and costume. Their faces are round, with big pointed ears, `skin-base` skin and a strong blush.

### Mr. Boom: the eldest

- **Character**: the leader. Proud and loud; he bellows, wails and announces. He speaks for Mr. Bim.
- **Look**: stocky, with black hair, heavy black brows and a wide grin.
- **Costume**: `boom-blue` shirt (the light is `boom-blue-light`, the folds `boom-blue-deep`); suspenders striped `boom-suspender` red and paper white, with gold clips; `boom-mustard` trousers; slouchy `boom-boot` purple boots. On the dusk pages the shirt is a paler blue-grey; use `boom-blue-light` with `paper-shade` there.

### Mr. Bam: the middle brother

- **Character**: grumpy and jealous of Boofo. He scowls, sniffs and complains, but he is sorry in the end.
- **Look**: slim, with dark tousled hair, round gold wire glasses (`bam-glasses`) and a frown.
- **Costume**: `bam-purple` smock coat with pencils in the breast pocket (`bam-purple-light`, `bam-purple-deep`); `bam-blue` trousers; `bam-boot-red` boots; purple earmuffs outdoors.

### Mr. Bim: the youngest

- **Character**: shy and timid. He whispers his ideas to Mr. Boom, loves Boofo first and cries when he is lost.
- **Look**: small, with orange-red `bim-hair`, freckled rosy cheeks and a gap-toothed smile.
- **Costume**: `bim-green` tunic laced at the neck, with `bim-green-lime` sleeves and light planes and `bim-green-deep` folds. His pointed `bim-hat` (`bim-hat-orange` on the shadow side) has a `bim-hat-band` cuff and a gold bell at the tip. Yellow pointed shoes. The colors of his hat vary from page to page in book one. Use yellow-orange with a green band as the standard.

## Other elves

The workshop crowd mixes the same palette in new combinations: yellow coats with red hats, teal boots, green tunics and striped stockings. Never repeat a brother's exact outfit, so the three leads stay recognisable.

## The reindeer

- `reindeer-fur` body, with a `reindeer-fur-light` muzzle and belly and `reindeer-fur-deep` shadows; dark antlers; red harness straps (`santa-red`).
- A `reindeer-saddle` green saddle-cloth with `reindeer-trim` gold trim and bells.
- Keep them clearly lighter and tanner than Boofo.
