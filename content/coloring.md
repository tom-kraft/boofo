# Coloring the line art

How to take a page of book-two ink line art to the finished book-one look. It is written for digital coloring (Photoshop, Procreate, Clip Studio or similar), but the order works the same with real markers.

## Set up the file

1. Scan or import the line art at 600 dpi or more, then clean it to pure `ink-line` black on white.
2. Put the line on its own layer at the top, set to Multiply. Color never goes above it.
3. Fill the canvas with `paper-white`. Paper is the brightest value on the page, and it is also what makes the snow, the fur and the highlights.
4. Make these layers, from the bottom up: **Background wash**, **Flats**, **Shadow**, **Deep**, **Light**, **Pencil texture**, **Glints**, then **Ink**.

## The passes, in order

1. **Flats**: fill every region with its base token from the Character guide (`boofo-coat`, `boom-blue`, `santa-red`, `skin-base`…). Don't fill anything that should be white; leave it as paper. Use a flat, hard-edged fill that runs under the ink line.
2. **Shadow**: pick one light direction for the whole page (book one mostly lights from the upper left) and lay the matching shadow token in marker-like strokes that follow the form. Choose a brush with a slightly streaky, transparent edge. Overlapping strokes should build up, as real markers do.
3. **Deep**: add the `…-deep` tokens only in the tightest creases and contact shadows.
4. **Light**: put the `…-light` tokens on the planes that face the light. On skin, add `skin-blush`: two round cheeks and the nose tip on every face.
5. **Pencil texture**: add loose diagonal colored-pencil hatching. Use `snow-lilac` in snow shadows, `mountain-lilac` on hills and a few fur flicks of `boofo-coat-light` on Boofo. Keep it subtle; it should only be visible up close.
6. **Glints**: scratch paper-white glints on noses, eyes, Boofo's ears, glasses, bells and metal.
7. **Background wash**: paint the setting palette (see below) in soft, pale, wet-edged washes. Stop the wash a `halo-gap` short of every figure's contour, and fade it to white toward the page edges and behind the text.

## Rules that keep book two looking like book one

- Characters are saturated and backgrounds are pale. If a background area competes with a figure, lighten it.
- White comes from paper. Never paint white on top, except for small glints.
- Snow is paper, `snow-shadow` and lilac pencil. Never grey, never cream.
- Every face gets the blush.
- Solid blacks stay ink black: Boofo's ears, the elves' hair, belts and boots.
- Fade the art to white behind text blocks. Text sits on near-white, never on a busy wash.
- A spot illustration on a white page needs a pale floor wash under its feet, faded out at the edges.
- Speed lines, wobble arcs and sound bursts are ink only. Bark bursts may be short `santa-red` dashes, as on page 13.

## Page checklist

- [ ] Boofo's coat is `boofo-coat`, his ears are black, and his white markings are on the muzzle, bib, belly, four paws and tail tip.
- [ ] Each brother is in his own costume colors: Boom blue and mustard, Bam purple with glasses, Bim green with red hair.
- [ ] One light direction across the page; shadows and lights agree with it.
- [ ] A halo gap between every figure and its background wash.
- [ ] The background wash fades to white behind the text and at the page edges.
- [ ] No neutral greys anywhere; whites are shaded with `paper-shade` or `boofo-white-shadow`.
- [ ] Compared side by side with the matching scene reference at the same viewing size.
